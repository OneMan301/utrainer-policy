About this policy

Utrainer is a fitness and wellness app provided by OneManLab. This policy covers the 4.9.12 closed-test build. It uses a local profile, not a cloud account. No email address or password is required to use it.

Information you enter

Your optional display name, age, sex, exercise-guide preference, height, weight, goals, experience, activity, equipment, schedule and safety answers are used on your device to create and display a starting plan. Workout logs, measurements, routines, food and water entries, settings and optional progress photos are also stored on your device. Old test profiles may contain an email address entered in an earlier build; it is not used to contact you or uploaded by this build.

Photos and device features

You choose whether to add progress photos. Camera and gallery access is used only after you choose the corresponding action. Only the photo you choose or capture is added to Utrainer. Camera capture can also save a separate copy in Pictures/Utrainer in your device gallery. Utrainer does not upload photos or provide AI-generated personal exercise images in this build. Reference images retained from earlier tests remain local until you delete the local data. Notification permission is optional and is used for scheduled workout reminders.

Sharing and external services

This build has no Utrainer backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cloud synchronization, payment processing or automated image-upload service. Utrainer does not send your profile, health entries, logs or photos to OneManLab or sell them. If you email support or send feedback, we receive the information you choose to send through your email provider. Do not send sensitive health information or photos unless necessary. Opening the public policy site uses GitHub Pages; GitHub processes standard web request information under its own privacy statement. Google Play and Android may process their own installation, testing or diagnostic information under their policies.

Storage and security

Records and photos are stored in the app’s local WebView storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox. This is not a separate encrypted medical vault. Use a device screen lock and avoid sharing an unlocked device. Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled in this build. Manual backup is optional: you choose a password and destination, and the export is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password-derived key. If you choose a cloud destination in the Android file picker, that provider handles the encrypted file under its own terms. Keep the password secure; we cannot recover it.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, clear Android app storage or uninstall Utrainer. Profile → Privacy and local data → Delete local profile and data removes the local profile, workout and routine records, measurements, food and water entries, app-stored photos and old personal-image references. It also cancels workout reminders. Delete progress photos removes only the copies stored inside Utrainer. Separate gallery photos and exported backups are not deleted: remove those yourself from the gallery or chosen backup destination. There is no server account to delete. Support messages are retained only while needed to handle the request, normally no longer than 90 days after it is resolved, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Your choices and changes

Progress photos, notification permission and manual backup are optional. You can change notification permission in Android settings and revise your plan or safety answers in the app. The closed test has no purchases or subscription charges. If a future release adds accounts, uploads, analytics or payments, its privacy policy and any required consent will be updated before those features are used. Utrainer provides general fitness guidance, not medical advice or a guaranteed outcome.
